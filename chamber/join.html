NP Membership Benefits

  • No membership fee.
  • Access to chamber networking events.
  • Access to selected business learning resources.

Bronze Membership Benefits

  • Access to chamber networking events.
  • Discounts on selected training sessions.
  • Eligibility for selected member promotions.

Silver Membership Benefits

  • All Bronze membership benefits.
  • Additional discounts on selected events and training.
  • Eligibility for a business spotlight feature.

Gold Membership Benefits

  • All Silver membership benefits.
  • Priority consideration for chamber business spotlights.
  • Access to selected premium networking and promotional opportunities.
