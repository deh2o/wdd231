Oilfield Digital & Safety Hub

Individual Website Project Plan

1. Site Name

Oilfield Digital & Safety Hub

This name describes a central online resource for people who want to learn about digital skills and workplace safety in the oil and gas industry. “Hub” communicates that visitors can find related learning resources in one place.

Optional domain: oilfieldskillsafety.org

2. Site Purpose

The Oilfield Digital & Safety Hub will provide beginner-friendly information about digital skills, technology careers, and basic workplace safety practices relevant to the oil and gas industry. Visitors will be able to explore learning resources, review information about digital career pathways, and submit questions or suggest topics through a contact form.

The website will be an educational resource. It will not replace formal safety training, professional advice, or an employer's safety procedures.

3. Scenarios

  • What digital and data skills could help me prepare for a technology career in the oil and gas industry?
  • Where can I find beginner-friendly learning resources about workplace safety in oil and gas?
  • How can I save a learning resource so I can return to it later?
  • How can I suggest a topic or ask a question about the information on this website?

4. Color Scheme

The site will use a restrained industrial-inspired palette. Navy will establish a professional identity, orange will draw attention to important actions, and light backgrounds will keep content easy to read.

Industrial Navy — #18364A

Used for the header, footer, headings, and primary buttons.

Safety Orange — #D97706

Used for accents, section borders, and important highlights.

Light Gray — #F4F6F8

Used as the main page background to create visual space.

Charcoal — #202B36

Used for body text to provide strong contrast on light surfaces.

5. Typography

The website will use two font families to keep the design consistent and readable.

  • Arial: Used for body text, navigation, forms, and general interface content because it is familiar and readable.
  • Georgia: Used for major headings to give the website a clear visual hierarchy and a more editorial feel.
